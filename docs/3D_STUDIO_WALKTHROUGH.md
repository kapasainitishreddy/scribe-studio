# Scribe Studio 3D Previs Walkthrough

Welcome to the 3D Studio in Scribe Studio.

## The Hero 3D Studio Interface
The 3D Studio provides a powerful web-native viewport for blocking out cinematic scenes.
![Hero 3D Studio](../../artifacts/screenshots/04-hero-3d-studio.png)

## Adding Actors and Props
You can easily drag and drop assets, including characters and procedural props.
![Add Soldier](../../artifacts/screenshots/05-add-soldier.png)
![Add Michelle](../../artifacts/screenshots/06-add-michelle.png)

## Editing and Manipulation
Select, duplicate, and delete objects with industry-standard keyboard shortcuts (Ctrl+D, Delete, Ctrl+Z, F).
![Duplicate](../../artifacts/screenshots/07-duplicate.png)

## Auto-Blocking
Use LLM intelligence to automatically read your script and block the scene with actors, lights, and cameras.
![Auto Block](../../artifacts/screenshots/10-auto-block.png)

## Saving Shots
Frame your shot perfectly using the viewport controls and save it to your project storyboard.
![Save Shot](../../artifacts/screenshots/11-save-shot.png)

## Storyboard Integration
Your captured frames automatically propagate back into your filmmaking workflow.
![Capture Frame](../../artifacts/screenshots/12-capture-frame.png)
